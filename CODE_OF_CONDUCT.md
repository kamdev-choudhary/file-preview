# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other side is acting in good
faith and is probably busy.

## What that means here

- Critique code, not people. "This leaks the object URL" is useful; "this is
  terrible" is not.
- Maintainers owe you a clear yes, no, or "not now" — not unlimited free work.
  A closed PR is not an insult.
- Harassment, personal attacks, and discrimination of any kind are not welcome,
  in issues, PRs, commit messages, or anywhere else connected to this project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via
[GitHub](https://github.com/kamdev-choudhary). Reports stay private. Depending on
severity the response ranges from a warning to a permanent block from the
project.

This is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), kept short
deliberately.
